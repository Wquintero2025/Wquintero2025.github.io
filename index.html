Welcome to Petco Clearlake

Healthy pets. Happy people. Stronger communities.

Petco is a pet health and wellness company that offers food, supplies, services, and support for pets and pet parents. This website presents the Petco Clearlake store in Houston, Texas, as a friendly and convenient place for pet care.

Why visit Petco Clearlake?

Full-Service Grooming

Professional grooming services help keep dogs and cats clean, comfortable, and healthy with baths, haircuts, and nail trims.

Dog Training Classes

Group and individual training sessions support better behavior and stronger relationships between pets and their families.

Adoption & Community Support

Petco Clearlake partners with local organizations to promote pet adoptions and support animal welfare in the Houston area.
