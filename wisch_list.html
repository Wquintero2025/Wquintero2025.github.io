My Travel Wishlist

Six destinations I want to visit. Each card shows a beautiful image, where it is in the world, and three activities I’d like to do there.

Travel Wishlist

Each destination includes country + three activities.

Ski in Colorado (Breckenridge)

Country: United States

  • Learn to ski on beginner-friendly slopes.
  • Ride the gondola and enjoy mountain views.
  • Warm up with hot chocolate in town.

Cartagena, Colombia

Country: Colombia

  • Walk colorful colonial streets in the Walled City.
  • Swim and snorkel at Islas del Rosario.
  • Watch the sunset from the city walls.

Grand Canyon

Country: United States

  • See sunrise at Mather Point.
  • Hike part of Bright Angel Trail.
  • Visit the geology museum outlooks.

Great Wall of China (Mutianyu)

Country: China

  • Walk a restored, less-crowded section.
  • Learn about its history and towers.
  • Ride the toboggan down for fun.

Dubai, UAE

Country: United Arab Emirates

  • Go up the Burj Khalifa observatory.
  • Watch the Dubai Fountain night show.
  • Desert safari with dune bashing.

Colombia Coffee Region

Country: Colombia

  • See the wax palms in Cocora Valley.
  • Tour coffee farms and tastings.
  • Visit colorful towns like Salento and Filandia.
