About Petco Clearlake

Petco is a national pet health and wellness retailer with many locations across the United States and Mexico. The company focuses on nutrition, veterinary care, grooming, training, and adoption support to help pets live better lives.

The Petco Clearlake store is located in the Clear Lake area of Houston, Texas. It provides a convenient place to buy high-quality food, toys, and supplies, while also offering services such as grooming, dog training, and adoption events.

Brief History of Petco

Petco began in 1965 as a mail-order veterinary supply business in San Diego, California. In 1979, the company changed its name to Petco and started to grow as a retail chain. Over the following decades, Petco expanded across the country and today operates a large network of pet care centers.

The company continues to develop new services, including veterinary clinics and community programs, while promoting responsible pet ownership and adoption.

Mission and Values

Petco’s mission is to improve the lives of pets, pet parents, and Petco partners. The company aims to be a trusted resource in pet care by offering integrated services, quality products, and expert guidance.

Core values include care, responsibility, community, and wellness. These values are reflected in everyday interactions with customers and their pets at the Clearlake location and beyond.
