Travel Wishlist

A collage of six places with image, name, and three reasons for each.

Ski in Colorado (Breckenridge)

  • Learn to ski on beginner‑friendly slopes.
  • Mountain views and winter town vibe.
  • Hot chocolate after a day on the snow.

Cartagena, Colombia

  • Walk colorful colonial streets in the walled city.
  • Swim at Islas del Rosario.
  • Caribbean sunsets on the ramparts.

Grand Canyon

  • See sunrise at Mather Point.
  • Short hike on Bright Angel Trail.
  • Epic views that photos can’t capture.

Great Wall of China (Mutianyu)

  • Walk a restored, less crowded section.
  • History and engineering in one view.
  • Ride the toboggan down for fun.

Dubai, UAE

  • See the city from Burj Khalifa.
  • Watch the fountain show at night.
  • Desert safari and dune bashing.

Colombia Coffee Region

  • See the wax palms in Cocora Valley.
  • Tour coffee farms and tastings.
  • Colorful towns like Salento and Filandia.
